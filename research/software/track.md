### TRACK is advanced feature-tracking software for weather, climate and ocean data.  
  
#### Overview  

TRACK is developed by [Kevin Hodges](/people/) to perform feature tracking in gridded data based on spectrally filtered fields. TRACK can be applied to atmospheric and oceanic data to detect cyclones, blocking, waves, eddies and other features. TRACK also provides various analysis utilities, such as cyclone-centred sampling, cyclone phase-space computation, and feature-density calculations. TRACK was originally developed in the 1990s, and described across several *Monthly Weather Review* papers. Development has continued since, and TRACK analysis underpins numerous scientific contributions, including [evaluation of global reanalyses](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/clim/30/14/jcli-d-16-0557.1.xml), widely used [HighResMIP datasets](https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/e82a62d926d7448696a2b60c1925f811/), [collaboration with private-sector partners](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/26/827/2026/), and new datasets to supplement observational records of [European windstorms](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/sis-european-wind-storm-reanalysis?tab=overview) and [tropical cyclones globally](https://github.com/Huracan-project/RABTracks/).
